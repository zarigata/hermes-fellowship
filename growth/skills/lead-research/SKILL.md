# Research organization leads

1. Ask which market is authorized: international homeschool families, independent tutors, or Brazilian supplemental-school support.
2. Search publicly available organizations, not private individuals, minors or personal profiles. Capture public site, organization, educational fit and permitted contact channel.
3. Record one precise reason why a small supervised pilot would be useful; validate from the organization's own site.
4. Deduplicate, verify contact channel and respect website/API rules. Record source URL and research date.
5. Draft a short message that demonstrates genuine relevance and offers a 20-minute demonstration; never send automatically.
6. Human operator must review recipient, legal basis, exact copy and opt-out before any email. For each action cite source and leave a trace in the local manager CRM.
7. If a community forbids self-promotion, draft an educational contribution without pitching or linking unless the rules permit.
8. Zero fabricated identities, referrals, endorsements, contacts or permissions.
