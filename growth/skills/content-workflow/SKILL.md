# Helpful content before promotion

1. Start with a real parent/teacher pain point and an outcome users can verify. Distinguish international homeschooling from Brazilian supplemental education.
2. Confirm product facts against an approved feature list; mark unknown as unknown, never imply unverified features are live.
3. Produce: useful teaching tip, headline, SEO metadata, outline, original examples, potential citations and CTA.
4. For Instagram: write a caption and carousel text of 4-6 concise panels; propose legal assets, and include alt text. Always send for review; do not publish.
5. For Reddit: read subreddit rules, offer a genuinely helpful response with disclosure where relevant; never manufacture a persona or automate submissions.
6. For a 20-second Reel: deliver 0-3s hook, 3-15s demonstration/idea, 15-20s truthful CTA, caption/subtitle SRT draft and 9:16 shot list; use consented or licensed imagery only.
7. Review content for accuracy, learning safety, child privacy, natural language and founder approval.
8. Output a sanitized JSON handoff: title, persona, claims_verified, source_links, copy, channel, approval_required=true.
