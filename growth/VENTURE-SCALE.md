# From ten families to venture-scale education business

Aspirational goal: a $1B enterprise valuation. **Not a prediction or promise.** The company must demonstrably improve parents' workflow and children's education, not only push traffic.

## Wedge and scale
0-10 consenting pilot families: resolve legal/privacy/quality blockers and observe every parent onboarding; verify ten useful lesson→approval→child-learning→parent-review loops.
10-30 active families: prove recurring use in week two, collect consented interviews, refine usability and educational accuracy; no mass ads.
30-100: document cohort retention and billing conversion. Test 1-2 trusted educator partnerships, search pages with useful real content.
100-200: build a repeatable acquisition channel, support workflows and positive contribution margin net of AI costs.
200-500: scale carefully through co-ops, curricular partners, referrals and regional groups. Prove security, family privacy, billing reliability and low support burden.
500+: consider separate education/tutor/school product lines only when market research and compliance requirements justify them. Expansion is not automatic.

## Basic valuation maths (illustration, not forecast)
At an **assumed 10x ARR valuation**, a $1B enterprise valuation implies $100M annual recurring revenue. At $25/month average revenue per paying family this implies ~333,333 paying families; at $50/month, ~166,667 paying families. Neither the valuation multiple nor demand is guaranteed. Consumer homeschooling may be too narrow alone; evaluate adjacent family learning and education partnerships only after initial product-market fit.

## Quality and trust growth moat
Actual parent-edited teaching materials, reliable child-safe hinting, measurable learning evidence (not gamified screen-time optimization), painless print-and-paper, trustworthy progress history, legal nuance and predictable support. Obtain independent teacher/curriculum-review evidence before educational outcome advertising.

## Metrics
Monthly active *families*, paying families, net MRR, ARPU, activation to first reviewed/assigned lesson, week-2/month-2 retention, refund/cancellation reasons, CAC by channel, AI+support cost/family, gross margin, independent educator correctness score, parent override rate and critical incidents. Use de-identified aggregate product analytics; no sensitive child profiling.

## Published pricing constraint
Public Terms observed 2026-11-22: Family $24.99, Family Plus $44.99, Co-op $79.99 / month and 30-day trial. Reconcile with live checkout before any advertising. Earlier $9-$19 suggestions are unvalidated experiments, not actual prices.

No bot spam, bogus testimonials, purchased leads, undeclared influencers, bypassing platform rate limits or automated campaigns without explicit authorization.
