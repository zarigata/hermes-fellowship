# SchoolBox homeschooling launch — 10 → 30 → 100 → 200 → 500

**North star:** 10 active families who repeatedly use the product and recommend it. Not 1,000 scraped email addresses.

## Product positioning

- **International homeschool offering:** parent's assistant for planning, assignments, safe child tutoring and progress visibility, where locally lawful. Never promise AI can replace parents/teachers.
- **Brazil offering:** supplemental learning / teacher-school support. Homeschooling is not a federally regulated alternative to standard school attendance under the STF's Tema 822 as of Nov 2026; get current legal review before claims.
- Clear separation of *public marketing* data from student/family educational data; do not feed actual student records into marketing models.

## Phase gates

| Active families | Objective | Minimum evidence | Allowed next spend |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Validate | 10 consenting pilot families, onboarding done, qualitative feedback, bugs prioritized | Mainly founder time |
| 10–30 | Learn | Weekly activation measured, 5 clear testimonials only with permission, retention trend | Small content budget |
| 30–100 | Repeat | Document one repeatable acquisition channel and reliable support workload | Small trials/paid acquisition |
| 100–200 | Improve unit economics | Subscription gross margin, support hours, churn and referral data | Broaden distribution |
| 200–500 | Scale carefully | Reliable uptime, billing support, data protection processes and sustained retention | More paid channels/partners |

No transition is automatic: if users do not finish first useful session or come back, improve the platform, not advertising volume.

## Pricing experiments — illustrative, NOT validated

- First 10 families: free, time-boxed 30-60 day pilot with disclosure and consent; collect feedback.
- At 30: a grandfathered founding-family option; test **$9 / $15 / $19** monthly family rates (example US pricing; decide after interviews and running-cost calculations), no trick subscriptions.
- At 100+: compare per-family vs per-child pricing; show parent/teacher controls, transparent AI usage caps and opt-out.
- School/municipality: separate quoted contracts, training, service levels and procurement processes. Never mix school child data into direct-response marketing.

Example at $15/month per paying family (assumes **all** active families pay, excludes transaction fees, AI, tax, support and churn): 30 = $450 MRR, 100 = $1,500, 200 = $3,000, 500 = $7,500. These are mathematical scenarios, not predictions.

## Weekly operating rhythm

- Monday: Manager reviews opted-in lead pipeline, replies, meeting schedule, conversion/retention; proposes at most 5 relevant contacts.
- Tuesday: Marketing writes one useful educational article, 3 short social drafts and 1 reel script, based only on verified product features and sources.
- Wednesday: Founder approves a small number of relevant outreach messages and one public post. Marketing revises instead of bulk-posting.
- Thursday: Founder/manager collect pilot feedback and resolve first-session failures.
- Friday: Review signups, activation, demos, cost, churn, support hours, and what to stop next week.

## KPIs

Lead source → qualified organization → approved contact → response → demo → opted-in trial → first successful lesson/assignment → week-2 retained family → paid family. Count at **family level**, not individual children. Use aggregated, privacy-protecting data. A small sample needs qualitative evidence, not over-precise percentage claims.

## Example non-spam outreach

Subject: Would a small trial help your learning group?

Hello [name], I run SchoolBox, an early-stage tool to help parents plan lessons and follow progress. I saw that [specific public organization detail]. If useful, could I show you a short demo and hear what doesn't work for your families? No obligation. If this isn't relevant, reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.

This is an example for targeted organizational outreach only, subject to local marketing law, actual research and approval. Include sender identity, physical mailing address and working opt-out route when required.
