## Score channels: 0-5 for each
Real parent/educator fit; permitted contact channel; clear problem/solution fit; legitimacy/trust; near-term ability to run a consensual supervised pilot. >=18/25 means **research**, not sending approval.

## Lightweight Atom cadence
Daily (short sequential run): summarize 1-3 public parent needs and draft a helpful answer; no automated posting.
Weekly: Manager investigates five organizational prospects and one partnership; Marketing produces one educator-reviewed guide, three short posts and one 20-second script. Provide founder approval queue.
Monthly: compare pricing, product gaps, public legal updates, search diagnostics, retention and support trends (only privacy-preserving aggregated product metrics).

Escalation: minor safety/privacy reports and legal risks immediately to founder, never first to marketing. Suspend planned content if platform API rules change. Use official API/RSS where authorized, never crawling behind logins. Keep source links, dates and uncertainties.
