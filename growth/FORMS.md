# Three forms to implement (not yet deployed)

## A. First ten parents: no-card pilot registration
Parent/legal guardian name and email; country and region (US/Canada state/province); role (parent/tutor/co-op organizer); family-size band 1/2/3+ (do NOT ask for child names, DOB or grades on a marketing form); main challenges (weekly planning, printing, grading, records, engagement, other); optional feedback. Separate *unchecked* boxes for trial communication and optional future marketing emails, with clear privacy/retention statement. Human review and rate limits; no hidden marketing consent.

## B. Parent learning interview
Ask: What does a typical homeschool morning look like? What are the three hardest tasks? What curriculum do you already use? How do you know your child learned a concept? Show us your first lesson generation/approval/printing attempt. Where did the AI get something wrong? What would you trust less about the product? Which feature is valuable enough to pay for? Why might you stop? May we contact you again?
Use adult feedback only; sanitize details and do not copy identifiable child information to marketing.

## C. Partnership contact
Organization name, public site, contact name and professional email, approximate families served, collaboration type (demo / co-hosted parent workshop / evaluation / co-op trial), permission to reply to this specific request, separate optional marketing opt-in. Add privacy notice, honest compensation disclosures and no fake reviews.

## Stored in Manager private CRM only
Source, verified public URL, permitted contact channel, consent legal basis, current status, follow-up consent, opted-out flag, demo, trial, first useful lesson activation, week-two retained, paid and estimated support cost. Keep child data and actual learning activity out of marketing storage; aggregate counts only.

## Proposed compelling CTA copy (subject to product verification)
'Your curriculum. Your teaching decisions. AI that assists.'
'Create a lesson you can edit before your child sees it.'
'Organize your homeschool week without handing education over to an AI.'

Do NOT publish a new form on the live website without product, privacy and legal review.
